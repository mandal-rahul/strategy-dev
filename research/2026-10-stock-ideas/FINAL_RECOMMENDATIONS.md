**India-specific rules**
- 20% TCS on LRS remittances above ₹10 lakh a year; it is refundable at filing, but the cash is locked up. Keeping this year's global purchases near ₹10 lakh avoids it.
- US estate tax of up to 40% applies to US-situs holdings above ~$60k (~₹58 lakh); there is no India–US estate treaty. Talk to an adviser before going above that.
- The US withholds 25% of dividends (claim credit on Form 67). Report all foreign assets in Schedule FA.
- Keep dry powder in rupees: Indian 91-day bills pay ~5.6% vs ~3.9% on US T-bills.
- The global sleeve is also a bet on a weaker rupee. A peace deal taking INR from ~96.7 back toward ~92 would cost ~4–6% in rupee terms.
