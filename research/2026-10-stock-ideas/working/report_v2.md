## The entry rule (applied to every name)

Every name below is judged attractive **at today's price** — no pick depends on waiting for a lower price. The only thing that varies is *how much to buy today*:
- **No scheduled binary event in the next ~3 weeks → buy the full position today.**
- **Results/IPO within ~3 weeks → buy half today and the rest immediately after the event, whatever the price** (Netflix: one-third, because its print is the most binary). This spreads event risk; it is not waiting for a dip. An investor who prefers simplicity can buy everything today and accept the event volatility.

### G1. Intuit (Nasdaq: INTU) — BUY now, full position. Conviction 4/5. Weight ~16%

- **Price:** $291.08 (Oct 6 intraday; Oct 9 close: see price table). 52w high ~$689–694 → ~58% below; 52w low ~$252.93 (Aug) → ~15% above the low. −53.5% YTD (Oct 8).
- **Valuation (corrected after red team):** from FY27 Intuit's non-GAAP EPS **includes** stock comp ($5.81/share). FY27 (to Jul-2027) guides: non-GAAP EPS $22.88–23.12 → ~12.7x; **GAAP EPS $20.12–20.36 → ~14.4x GAAP P/E**. On the old (SBC-excluded) basis the guide is ~$28.7–28.9, i.e. ~10x. FY26 GAAP EPS was $16.46; FY26 non-GAAP (old basis) $24.27 (+20%).
- **Why it fell (corrected):** the August "EPS miss" vs the $27.30 consensus was mostly the SBC accounting change; the real disappointment was **revenue: FY27 +9–10% vs +14% in FY26** and below the ~$23.72B consensus (Mailchimp weakness, lower revenue per TurboTax customer). Also: US TurboTax units −2% to 39M in the 2026 season ("lost on price" with filers under $50k); a 17% workforce cut; a securities class action over TurboTax statements; the broad "AI agents replace TurboTax/QuickBooks" narrative.
- **Why it is mispriced:** ~14x GAAP earnings for a franchise still guiding ~10% revenue growth and 13–14% growth in its small-business platform (QuickBooks/GBS), with very high returns on capital. Regulated, liability-bearing tax/accounting workflows with deep data integration are the hardest software moats for AI agents to break quickly.
- **Bear evidence we accept:** the low end of TurboTax is genuinely eroding (units −2%); this is a slower grower than its old multiple implied. That is why the thesis is "re-rating from ~14x GAAP", not "back to the old highs".
- **What would prove us wrong:** FQ1 FY27 guidance cut (~late Nov); QuickBooks Online subscriber/ARPC deceleration; a second year of TurboTax unit declines in the 2027 season.
