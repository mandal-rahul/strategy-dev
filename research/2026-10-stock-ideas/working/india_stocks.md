- NIM compression, plus a high loan-to-deposit ratio after the merger.
- CEO succession uncertainty.
- Market-wide risk-off from the West Asia war.

**Management change (the key catalyst)**
- Sashidhar Jagdishan declined a third term; he told the board on 29-Aug-26. He retires on 26-Oct-26.
- The RBI approved **Anup Bagchi** (ex-ICICI group; MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life) as MD & CEO for 3 years from **27-Oct-26**.
- On 12-Sep-26 the board also appointed two whole-time directors and created an executive-director role.

Sources: [Business Standard ~1-Oct-26](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/people/hdfc-bank-board-appoints-anup-bagchi-as-md-ceo-with-effect-from-october-27-126100101096_1.html), [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/news/hdfc-bank-approves-new-ceo-candidates-seeks-rbi-approval-for-board-expansion).

**Why it may be mispriced**
- The franchise is still growing loans in the mid-teens and profit is still growing.
- The overhang is mostly governance and leadership noise. The governance part was investigated and not substantiated, and the leadership part is now resolved by the RBI's approval of a new CEO.
- The P/B of ~1.8x is low for India's largest private bank. Its 5-year median P/B is not verified here; from memory I would put it well above 2.5x.

**Brokerage views**
- **Jefferies (late Sep-26): Buy, base case ₹880 (about 20% upside), bull case ₹935.** Jefferies says a re-rating hinges on the CEO transition and execution ([BS 28-Sep-26](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/hdfc-bank-s-rerating-hinges-on-ceo-transition-and-execution-jefferies-126092800492_1.html)).

- Morgan Stanley and CLSA: not verified.

**Catalysts**
- New CEO takes charge on 27-Oct-26, followed by his first strategy commentary.
- Q2 FY27 results: **date not verified**. Historically HDFC Bank reports in the third week of October.
- NIM trajectory as deposits reprice.

**Risks and what would prove the thesis wrong**
- NIM keeps falling below about 3.2%.
- Deposit growth stalls, forcing loan growth to slow.
- The new CEO "kitchen-sinks" provisions or resets guidance.
- The US class action escalates, or new governance findings emerge.

- Large-deal TCV falls below about $2 bn per quarter.
- CC growth turns negative.
- Margins break below 20%.
